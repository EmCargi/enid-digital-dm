# Tavarre Outpost

**Type:** Neutral arcology corridor — the contested middle
**Population:** ~15,000 — mixed LOA/Alliance/Tavarre civilians, both recruiters, one cult
**Hook:** The 3-node starter's hub (node_01)

The last neutral dome in the Tavarre corridor. Both sides claim it, neither holds it. LOA's recruiter offers psycho-frame training; the Alliance liaison offers hazard pay and a clean uniform; the fixer in the back knows the gate is in the storm and the election was rigged.

**Map:**

```
[Noa Enid] —— Tavarre Outpost —— [Akronia]
                 |
            Storm Wall (hazard)
                 |
            Hurricane Gate (Wayline to Bazaroth) + Psycho-Frame Duel arena
```

**Validates as:** `node_type: hub` or `entrance` in the starter labyrinth.
