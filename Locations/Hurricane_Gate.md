# Hurricane Gate — Wayline to Bazaroth

**Type:** World gate (permanent hurricane, Ch.14 p.327)
**Wayline:** Enid ↔ Bazaroth (one of Enid's 7 waylines; Enid has no gate to Ikaris)
**Activation:** Penetrate the eye + wish to be elsewhere (Ch.14 gate rule, p.309)

One of Enid's gates is *the storm itself* — a permanent hurricane whose eye is the Wayline. Mecha that punch through the wall and wish find themselves elsewhere: usually Bazaroth, if they know the Key.

**Known to:** LOA's hereditary gatekeeper (Neo-Logos chief scientist) — who quietly sends forces to secure "unimportant ruins" that are actually gates. Most Enid citizens are ignorant of the Cosmic Web (Ch.14 p.327).

**Skeleton Key:** The Tavarre Skeleton Key (starter roster) can operate this gate and can permanently destroy it by passing through — the campaign's lock/destroy choice.
