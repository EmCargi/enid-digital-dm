# Waylines, Gates & Keys — Enid's Slice of the Cosmic Web

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 309–311 (Cosmic Web) + Enid p.327.

## What Applies on Enid

- **7 waylines:** 6 to Prime Worlds (all save Ikaris), 1 secret to Omphalos (Earth's thrones), plus the unusable detached wayline — Enid is a hub, not a backwater.
- **Dozens of gates:** Most buried in storm-flattened ruins, some safe inside arcologies/domes. One is the hurricane itself.
- **Keys:** Each gate has a Key — soul-attuned, can lock/open the gate, gate destroyed if Key ritually killed on it. Most Enid citizens don't know they *are* Keys.
- **Skeleton Keys:** Can operate *any* gate, detect nearby gates, pick any lock (physical or password). The Tavarre Skeleton Key in the starter roster is one — hunted, mobile, the campaign's master key.
- **Way-Bound:** Demons/asrai summonable via true-name ritual at the hurricane gate. Bazaroth cults use this.

## The Gatekeeper

Enid's hereditary gatekeeper is the Neo-Logos chief scientist — who uses LOA military deployments to secure gates under the cover of "unimportant ruins." The party may be sent to secure a gate without being told it's a gate.

## Mechanics Hooks

- **Gate activation:** Touch + wish to be elsewhere (Ch.14 p.309). In a module: `required_check: {stat: stat_soul, skill: "Occult", dv: 16, fail_damage: 0}` — success = transit, fail = storm exposure.
- **Skeleton Key choice:** Lock the hurricane gate (seal Bazaroth, lose the Wayline) or destroy it by passing through (permanent, needs the Key).
- **Omphalos:** 7 thrones, 7 doors, 6 seated gatekeepers + empty Earth throne. Not in the starter — the demo disc hub for later.
