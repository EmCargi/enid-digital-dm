# Noa Enid

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 326–327. The first arcology, founded by Neo-Logos.

## Overview

Before the storm, Neo-Logos built a psychic academy that found and trained psychics (ESP, telekinesis, precognition). When their precogs saw the hyper-storm coming, the world's governments scoffed — so Neo-Logos built **Noa Enid**, the underground city that became the template for every arcology that followed.

Today Noa Enid is the capital of the League of Arcologies (LOA), dominated by psychics and the scientists of Neo-Logos. It is both a refuge and a laboratory.

## Key Elements

- **Psychotronic cyber-machines:** The "psycho-frame" mecha — humanoid, 15–30m, amplifies the pilot's psychic aura 10x (force field, gravity neutralisation, storm-drawn lightning). The signature tech of LOA. See `Mechanics/Psycho_Frames.md`.
- **The breeding program:** Psychic gifts are not genetic, but Neo-Logos believes they can be stimulated by early telepathic exposure (even in the womb). The hereditary gatekeeper of Enid is a chief Neo-Logos scientist, quietly directing LOA policy to secure gates.
- **Hyper-psychic gestalt:** The long-term project — a mass of linked psychics large enough to will the storm calm. The reason LOA recruits every psychic it can find.

## Adventure Use

Noa Enid is the **LOA quest hub** — where the party is recruited, framed, or assigned to Tavarre. The gatekeeper's secret (they know the wayline map) is the lever that turns a local war story into a Multiverse one.
