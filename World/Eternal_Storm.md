# Eternal Storm

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 326–327 (Enid). 70 years AES — After the Eternal Storm.
> First-party canon, hand-authored vault page.

## Overview

The Eternal Storm is not a single hurricane but a **planetary collapse** — hundreds of category-seven hyper-storms, relentless, world-spanning, born from pollution-induced global warming that Neo-Logos psychics predicted and the world ignored. Crops failed, millions fled, arcologies went up, war followed.

On Enid today the sky is a weapon. Helicopters are useless. Infantry moves in heavy armor. Cities live underground, behind domes, or on the ocean floor. The storm is the dungeon.

## Mechanics Hooks

- **Heavy Weather as hazard:** Every outdoor node outside an arcology carries an environmental check (Mind or Soul, DV 12–16, fail = Energy drain, knockback, or `Defect: Wind Vulnerability` trigger).
- **Calm windows:** The `besm-environmental-hazards-ledger` window — a few hours of calm per week when travel and combat are possible. Miss it and you're fighting the sky.
- **Storm gates:** One permanent hurricane on Enid is a **world gate** (Wayline to Bazaroth). Mecha that penetrate its eye and wish to be elsewhere trigger the gate (Ch.14 p.327).

## Factions' Answers

| Faction | Answer to the Storm |
|---|---|
| **LOA / Neo-Logos** | A hyper-psychic gestalt — enough linked psychics to calm the weather by will |
| **Global Alliance** | Project Butterfly — fusion orbital lasers heating air masses, guided by chaos math (and now, stolen/cybernetic psychics) |
| **Tavarre neutrals** | Endure — Tavarre's geography makes it ideal for satellite launches, so everyone wants it |

## Adventure Use

The storm is the **timer and the terrain**. Every module outside an arcology should have at least one storm node. The hurricane-gate is the Cosmic Web hook — the first time the players find it, Enid stops being a local war and becomes a Multiverse door.
