# Global Alliance

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 326–327. Led by the Chairman General, strongest power: Republic of Akronia.

## Overview

The Global Alliance formed from the surviving governments that *didn't* follow Neo-Logos into the arcologies. It claims to stand for "normal humans" against the psiocracy of LOA. Some of its demagogues blame the Eternal Storm on Neo-Logos experiments, fanning anti-psychic prejudice (mind-reading, will-sapping).

Underneath the rhetoric, the Alliance has its own plan — and it's willing to enslave psychics to get it.

## Key Elements

- **Project Butterfly:** Fusion-powered weather-control satellites firing orbital lasers to heat and steer air masses. The name from chaos theory (a butterfly's wings can make a hurricane). Requires supernatural prediction accuracy — Alliance computers failed, so they're now hunting psychics to guide the lasers.
- **Psycho-slaves:** The Alliance's answer to the psycho-frame. A two-crew mecha: a normal command pilot + a **psycho-pod** where a captured psychic's power is drained by cybernetic link. Most "pilots" are not volunteers. See `Mechanics/Psycho_Frames.md`.
- **Tank superiority:** Before psycho-frames, the Alliance's tanks and APCs dominated — larger, newer, more numerous than LOA's older armor. The mecha war made it a stalemate.

## The Tavarre War

Two years ago LOA and the Alliance both courted the unaligned nation of **Tavarre** — ideal satellite-launch geography + large psychic population. When Tavarre's pro-LOA party won by a slim margin, the opposition cried psychic voter manipulation, riots followed, a fractured coup, and both superpowers sent troops. The war has lasted 3+ years, now a bloody stalemate.

## Adventure Use

The Alliance is the **lawful-evil mirror** — understandable fear of psychic power turned into atrocity. An Alliance psycho-slave rescue is an instant moral hook.
