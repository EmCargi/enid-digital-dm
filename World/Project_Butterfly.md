# Project Butterfly — The Hyper-Psychic Gestalt

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 326–327 + `ideas/besm-starter-info.md` (author's canonical merge).
> **Correction (2026-09-12):** Project Butterfly *is* the Hyper-Psychic Gestalt — Neo-Logos' clandestine genetic project to merge all living psychics into one mind capable of pacifying the Eternal Storm. Not a separate Alliance laser program (that reading was drift; corrected to the ideas author's intent).

## Overview

Project Butterfly is Neo-Logos' long game: merge every Homo Psyche on Enid — every cultivated child, every academy graduate, every Tavarre psychic both sides hunt — into a **Hyper-Psychic Gestalt** that can will the storm calm. The name is chaos theory made literal: enough linked minds, precisely tuned, can still a hurricane.

The LOA's public story (arcology survival) and the Alliance's stolen-psychic "psycho-slave" program are both downstream of this secret. The Alliance isn't building its own gestalt — it's *stealing the gestalt's nodes*.

## The Secret

The gestalt is untested. Who knows what a merged mind that powerful *is*? And it needs *everyone* — which is why every Tavarre psychic matters, why the breeding program exists, and why the election fraud was worth the war.

## Mechanics Hooks

- **Gestalt node as contested resource:** Every Tavarre town has 1–2 hidden psychics; both sides hunt them. The party's choice determines whether the gestalt grows or is crippled.
- **Orbital strike (legacy reading) — deprecated:** The laser-correction check is now alternate lore; the storm is calmed by gestalt will, not lasers.

## Adventure Use

Butterfly is the **secret and the timer** — LOA says 10 more years; Bazaroth cults say open the gate before it wakes.
