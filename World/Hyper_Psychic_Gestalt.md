# Hyper-Psychic Gestalt

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 326–327. LOA / Neo-Logos long-term project.

## Overview

Neo-Logos believes psychic gifts can be cultivated — not inherited, but *stimulated* by early telepathic contact, even in the womb. Give enough infants that nudge, raise them in the academy, link them, and eventually you'll have a **hyper-psychic gestalt**: a mass of psychics whose combined will can do what no single mind can — calm the Eternal Storm and restore Enid.

It's a beautiful, terrifying idea. Who knows what a gestalt mind that powerful would *be*?

## The Cost

- The breeding program is generations long and ethically grey — infants as experiments.
- The gestalt itself is untested. The Alliance calls it a monstrous power that could be worse than the storm.
- It competes directly with Project Butterfly for the same resource: psychics.

## Adventure Use

The gestalt is the **long game** — a background project the party can support, sabotage, or replace. It also explains why every Tavarre psychic matters: each one is a future node in the gestalt (or a laser guide, depending on who gets them).
