# Major Kaelen Vance

**Faction:** Global Alliance — Republic of Akronia, Chairman General's command
**Role:** Frontline Armored — heavy infantry / psycho-frame hunter
**Age:** 38

## Appearance

Broad, scarred, buzz-cut gone grey at the temples. Heavy plating AR 15 over Alliance fatigues, hydraulic braces at the knees. Voice like a dome door closing.

## Personality

Pragmatic, loyal, hardware-faithful. Anti-psi by doctrine but not by cruelty — he'll work with a psychic if it keeps his people alive. Believes the Alliance's cause is order, not hate. Hates waste.

## Background

Career Alliance armor. Fought the Tavarre War from the first month — led tanks through the Storm Wall before psycho-frames made them obsolete. Now hunts LOA frames with an old chassis and a crew that trusts him. Stationed at Tavarre Outpost to hold the neutral corridor and keep the cults out of the gate.

## Abilities

Hardware-dependent frontline — suppressive fire, hydraulic stance, pod extraction. Hardboiled, Brutal. Military 3, Mechanics 2. The frame is his home; without it, he's exposed.
