# Orin Drake

**Faction:** Bazaroth — storm cult, abyssal-touched
**Role:** Fanatical Infiltrator — arc discharge, lightning step
**Age:** 31

## Appearance

Storm-burned, abyssal runes crawling up the neck, eyes that reflect lightning before it strikes. Wears cult leathers under a re-purposed Alliance coat.

## Personality

Zealous, hungry, convinced the Eternal Storm is a feeding ground — and he is right. Sees the hurricane gate as a mouth to be opened. Fanatical Zeal makes him brilliant and brittle: when the ritual holds, he's unstoppable; when disrupted, the wild tempest consumes him.

## Background

A Tavarre native who walked into the hurricane gate as a child and came back wrong — marked, lightning-touched, hearing Bazaroth on the wind. Built a cult in the storm wall's shadow, carving the gate's Key name into black glass. Knows the Way-Bound true names that can be called at the gate. Wants the gate open, not locked.

## Abilities

Lightning Weapon 3, Immunity (Electricity) 1. Defect: Marked (Abyssal runes — detectable by psychics, anathema to Aradian wards). Lethal Blow when the ritual is active.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Fanatical Zeal — disruption of his ritual unravels him.
- **Sixth Guard:** Void Exposure — ritual disrupted → consumed by wild tempest.
- **Levers:** Arc Discharge / Lightning Step / Fanatic Flare
