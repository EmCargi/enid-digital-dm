# Vaelen Kor

**Faction:** LOA / Neo-Logos (Homo Psyche)
**Role:** Psychic Skirmisher — psycho-frame pilot trainee
**Age:** 22

## Appearance

Lean, storm-grey eyes, close-cropped hair bleached by dome-light. Wears the slate LOA pilot suit with psychotronic dampeners at the temples. Moves like someone listening to wind that isn't there.

## Personality

Earnest, observant, restless. Trained since childhood by Neo-Logos to believe his gift is a duty. Believes in the gestalt but fears what it will cost him.

## Background

Raised in Noa Enid's academy — exposed in utero to telepathic fields as part of Neo-Logos' cultivation program. Flagged as Homo Psyche at age 4 when he stopped a falling gantry with raw kinesis. Assigned to Tavarre Outpost for his first field posting: learn the storm, learn the gate, earn his frame.

## Abilities

Psionic skirmisher — vector kinesis and pulse barriers, evasive drift in the psycho-frame's 10x aura. Lightning Reflexes and Precise Aim when channeled through the frame. Telekinesis, Force Field, Mind Shield.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Psionic Burnout — pushing his gift too hard blanks his field; the frame goes dead weight.
- **Sixth Guard:** Resonance Feedback — overdrive that drops EP to 0 triggers catatonic collapse; the frame locks, the pilot goes dark.
- **Levers:** Vector Kinesis / Pulse Barrier / Evasive Drift
