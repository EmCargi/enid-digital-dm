# Unit 7-Nil "Clover"

**Faction:** Global Alliance — Involuntary Psycho-Slave (captive Homo Psyche)
**Role:** Trapped Psychic — enervation blasts, telepathic distress
**Age:** 19

## Appearance

Small, shaved head with collar scars, eyes that flicker with drained light. The psycho-pod harness has left hard lines at wrists and throat. Barely speaks above a whisper.

## Personality

Quiet, frayed, desperately lucid. A powerful psychic (Mind 8) bolted into a cockpit pod, power drained to run a warmachine she never volunteered to crew. Sends beacons when she can — mind shivers, pod static, a desperate signal that someone is still in there.

## Background

Identified as Homo Psyche as an infant in an Alliance territory — taken, not recruited. Surgically collared with a neural inhibitor, slotted into a psycho-slave frame where a command pilot makes the checks and she pays the cost. The frame's 10x amplifier runs on her. Every activation is a theft. Stationed at Tavarre Outpost because the Alliance needs its laser guidance there.

## Abilities

Enervation Blast, Telepathy 2. Defects: Involuntary Servitude, Fragile 2. The moral choice of the starter — free her (lose the frame) or use her (carry the Defect).

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Neural Inhibitor Collar — command overload tightens the collar; resistance risks seizure.
- **Sixth Guard:** Synaptic Snapping — command overload → violent psionic seizure; the pod locks, the frame convulses.
- **Levers:** Mind Shiver / Pod Static / Desperate Beacon
