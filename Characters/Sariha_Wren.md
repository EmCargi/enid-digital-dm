# Sariha Wren

**Faction:** Tavarre Neutral — fixer, smuggler, survivor
**Role:** Infiltrator — bribe routes, shadow slips, dead drops
**Age:** 29

## Appearance

Sharp, tired eyes, jacket with too many pockets, boots that have crossed the storm wall more than once. Always has a second way out.

## Personality

Wry, collateral-averse, loyal to debts. Survived the Tavarre presidential election — saw the ballot fraud, the psychic nudge, the riots, the coup that split the country. Now she trades in secrets and extractions, trying to keep neutrals alive in a proxy war.

## Background

Born in Tavarre, raised in the trade station's Eye. Ran logistics for the pre-war government, then watched Neo-Logos agents nudge the vote and the Alliance answer with tanks. Now she runs the outpost's shadow economy: bribe routes through the domes, dead drops in the storm, assets compromised and moved. Knows the hurricane gate is real because she's seen mecha vanish into it.

## Abilities

Infiltrator tradecraft — concealment, portable armoury, street-born cunning. Street 3, Business 2, Detective 2. Defect: Wanted 1 (both sides have a file on her).

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Collateral Aversion — civilian casualties freeze her; she'll abort a win to prevent them.
- **Sixth Guard:** Asset Compromise — secret revealed → unconditional surrender to protect her network.
- **Levers:** Bribe Route / Shadow Slip / Dead Drop
