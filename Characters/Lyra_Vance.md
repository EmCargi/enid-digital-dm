# Lyra Vance

**Faction:** Noa Enid — Homo Psyche, hereditary gatekeeper
**Role:** Support — harmonic wards, pain siphon, gate resonance
**Age:** 27

## Appearance

Soft-spoken, steady hands, the kind of person who stands between you and the wind. Wears the pale Noa Enid support mantle with a gate-key etched on the sternum.

## Personality

Empathic, self-sacrificing to a fault. Will take your wound into herself before she lets you fall. The gatekeeper secret weighs on her — she knows where the hurricane gate is, what Keys can do, and that every deployment to "secure an unimportant ruin" is a gate mission in disguise.

## Background

Hereditary gatekeeper of Enid — the Neo-Logos chief scientist's quiet heir. Trained as a Homo Psyche support: Sixth Sense (World Gates), Healing 2, Exorcism 1. Knows the breeding program, the gestalt plan, the missing Earth gatekeeper (Ozymandius), and the fact that the Enid gate to Bazaroth is the storm itself. Stationed at Tavarre Outpost to watch the gate and the Skeleton Key.

## Abilities

Sixth Sense (World Gates), Healing 2, Exorcism 1. Skills: Domestic 2, Academic 2. The party's healer and gate-reader — and the one who can tell a Skeleton Key from a normal Key.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Empathic Hemorrhage — ally's pain floods her; she overextends to siphon it.
- **Sixth Guard:** Soul Exhaustion — ally enters death check → collapses sharing the wound; the healer becomes the patient.
- **Levers:** Harmonic Ward / Pain Siphon / Gate Resonance
