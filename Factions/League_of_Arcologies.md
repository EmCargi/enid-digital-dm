# League of Arcologies (LOA)

**Leader:** Councils of Noa Enid (psychic-dominated) + Neo-Logos scientists
**Stronghold:** Noa Enid (first arcology) + allied arcologies
**Doctrine:** Psychotronic tech, psychic recruitment, hyper-psychic gestalt
**Gatekeeper:** Hereditary, embedded in Neo-Logos; secretly directs LOA to secure gates

LOA believes the storm is a psychic wound. Its answer is more psychics, better linked, until the weather obeys. Noble, patient, and willing to treat children as future batteries.

**Forces:** Psycho-frame mecha (15–30m, 10x aura amplifier, flight + psycho-dynamic shield + storm-lightning), older tanks/APCs, psychic infantry.
