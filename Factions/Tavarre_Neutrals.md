# Tavarre Neutrals

**Status:** Fractured neutral nation, flashpoint of the Tavarre War
**Why it matters:** Ideal satellite-launch geography + large psychic population — both sides need it for their storm fix.

Tavarre was not aligned until 3 years ago. Both superpowers courted its politicians; the pro-LOA presidential win (slim margin, cries of psychic voter fraud) triggered riots, an attempted coup, and the fracture. Now Tavarre is a patchwork of LOA-held north, Alliance-held south, and a contested corridor where the party operates.

**Use in the starter:** Tavarre Outpost — the neutral hub where LOA and Alliance recruiters share a dome and the hurricane gate hums.
