# Bazaroth Cults

**Source:** Cosmic Web (Enid has a hurricane gate to Bazaroth, Ch.14 p.327)
**Nature:** Demonic infiltration via desperation cults

Enid's tech and psychics make it resistant to full Bazaroth invasion, but the war and the storm make people desperate — cults spring up promising protection, power, or an end to the weather. A few demon lords see Enid as a farm for life-force, blaming their ravages on the war.

**Mechanics:** Bazaroth demons are *Way-Bound* — summonable via true-name ritual at the hurricane gate. A cultist who learns the gate's Key can call them. Skeleton Keys can lock or destroy the gate.

**Use in the starter:** The cult is the third faction in the Tavarre Outpost — the one that knows the gate is real and wants to open it.
